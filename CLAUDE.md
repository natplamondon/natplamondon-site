# Nat Plamondon Personal Site

## Project
Personal website for Nat Plamondon, Associate Director of Product Marketing. Built with Astro. Hosted on Vercel via GitHub.

## Purpose
Thought leadership and professional credibility. Not a job hunting site — no "open to work" language anywhere.

## Design system
- Background: #1E2620 (charcoal green)
- Surface: #242D26
- Text: #F0EBE1
- Muted text: #B8B0A4
- Accent: #C8922A (amber) — links, active states
- Accent secondary: #7A9E7E (sage) — case study category tags only
- Border: #2E3830
- Headlines: Syne 700
- Body: Lora 400
- Section labels: Lora italic 400, sentence case, not uppercase
- Max width: 780px centered

## Tone
Confident, direct, no fluff. The thinking is the product.

## Content
- Hero positions Nat around "companies that outgrew their story" — portfolio complexity, not credentials
- Three case studies: (1) From Learning Suite to Workforce Agility Platform, (2) Workforce Readiness: When the Market Shifts Under You, (3) Making Two Acquisitions Make Sense: Edcast & SkyHive
- Key metrics embedded in copy: 440% increase in multi-product adoption (case study 1), ~100% YoY SkyHive growth in first year as part of Cornerstone (case study 3)

## Rules
- Never change copy without being explicitly asked
- When given exact copy to place, use it verbatim — no rewriting, paraphrasing, or adjustment
- Never alter specific statistics or metrics in existing copy
- Never add decorative elements
- Always keep the container centered at 780px max-width
- Section labels are Lora italic, sentence case, not uppercase
